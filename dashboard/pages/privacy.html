This Privacy Policy explains how FreeSurf collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use our apps, websites, and services. FreeSurf provides free utility tools including invoice generation, link-in-bio pages, social media cross-posting, text-to-speech reading, and related services.

1. Information We Collect

Without an account: Most FreeSurf tools work without creating an account. When you use tools without signing in, your data (invoice drafts, generated audio, saved recordings, transcriptions) is stored locally on your device. We do not collect or have access to this locally stored data.

With an account (optional): If you choose to create an account, we collect:

  • Email address and authentication credentials (via Supabase) — collected when you create an account or sign in
  • Invoice drafts, business profiles, client information, and saved invoices you choose to sync to your account
  • Link-in-bio profile content including display name, bio, links, and avatar images
  • Social media account connections and OAuth tokens for cross-posting (Post tool)
  • Support requests and correspondence

If you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive the email address associated with that account and use it to recognize you and, if you opt in, to send the Feedfree Digest or product updates. We do not need your full name to operate the service, so we avoid storing additional profile details (such as your name or photo) from these sign-in providers where possible.

AI Processing: Some FreeSurf tools use AI to generate results. Before any of your content is sent to an AI provider, the app asks for your permission and explains what is being sent. FreeSurf is currently subscription-first and does not run third-party advertising. AI content is sent only to the provider required to produce the result you requested and is transmitted through our own servers.

FreeSurf uses hosted AI inference providers, currently Together AI (Together Computer, Inc., together.ai), and may also use OpenRouter or our own self-hosted RunPod GPU infrastructure; we generally use open-source models. Depending on the app, the following is sent to Together AI strictly to produce the output:

  • Transcriber: your audio is sent to be transcribed into text.
  • Natural Reader: your text is sent to be synthesized into speech audio.
  • Calorie Tracker: your meal photos are sent to estimate their nutrition.

Content is shared only with the provider running the model for that request, used solely to complete the request, not stored after processing, and never used to train or fine-tune models. Together AI processes this content only to generate the requested output and provides data protection that meets or exceeds our own standards. For full details on providers, inputs, and what happens to results, see our AI Processing Disclosure.

2. How We Use Information

  • Provide, maintain, and improve our tools and services
  • Authenticate users who choose to create accounts
  • Sync your data across devices when signed in
  • Respond to support requests
  • Send optional product updates or newsletters (only if you opt in)
  • Maintain security and prevent abuse

We do not sell personal information. We do not use your invoice data, link profiles, posts, or AI-generated content for advertising purposes.

3. Advertising, Tracking & Attribution

FreeSurf is currently a subscription-first product. The AI utility apps do not run third-party advertising, do not include advertising SDKs, and do not collect or use the Apple identifier for advertisers (IDFA) or the Android Advertising ID (AAID). We do not perform cross-app tracking, and therefore we do not show an Apple App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt.

For marketing measurement we rely on privacy-preserving, aggregate attribution (such as Apple's SKAdNetwork) that does not require personal data or tracking consent.

If we later introduce an ad-supported tier, this section will be updated before any advertising SDK is added, and any required tracking consent will be requested at that time.

4. Usage Analytics

To understand how our tools are used and to improve them, we may collect product analytics about how you interact with an app — for example, which screens and features you use, approximate session length, device type and operating system, and crash/performance diagnostics. This analytics data is tied to an anonymous or pseudonymous identifier and is not linked to your name or email.

We run our analytics on self-hosted, open-source software (PostHog) on our own infrastructure. As a result, usage/interaction data is not shared with a third-party analytics vendor. We do not send your content (text, audio, photos, invoices, transcripts, or posts) to analytics, and we do not record your screen or capture sensitive content for analytics purposes.

You can opt out of analytics where the app provides that setting; otherwise, analytics uses the same ad-tracking/privacy settings described above where applicable.

5. Third-Party Services

FreeSurf uses the following infrastructure and service providers:

  • Supabase — authentication and account data storage
  • Cloudflare — web hosting, Workers, R2 storage, and KV
  • Together AI / OpenRouter — hosted AI inference providers
  • RunPod — our own GPU infrastructure for self-hosted AI models

These providers process data on our behalf to deliver specific parts of the service.

6. Data Retention & Your Choices

  • Local data: Data stored on your device remains until you delete it or uninstall the app.
  • Account data: Retained while your account is active. You can delete your account by contacting us.
  • AI processing: Submitted text, audio, and photos are sent to the active AI inference provider to produce a result and are not retained there beyond the request. Generated results are saved locally on your device; if you are signed in, they may also be stored in our database and associated with your account for cross-device access. See our AI Processing Disclosure.
  • No model training: User content processed by our AI models is never used to train, fine-tune, or improve the models. Transcripts, audio, and images are used strictly for immediate real-time output.
  • Support requests: May be retained to resolve issues and document outcomes.

7. GDPR & CCPA Rights

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA) or California, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to access: Request a copy of personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to deletion: Request deletion of your account and associated data.
  • Right to opt out: Opt out of personalized advertising via your device's privacy settings or by limiting ad tracking.
  • Right to data portability: Request your data in a structured, machine-readable format.

To exercise these rights, contact us at support@freesurf.tools. We will respond within 30 days. For EEA users, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

8. Children's Privacy

FreeSurf is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy. Material changes will be noted with an updated effective date.
